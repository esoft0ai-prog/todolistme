Camplo Help Center

Camplo watches every lead from the moment it's born until someone on your team responds — and tells you, in plain words, what's slipping and what to do about it.

What Camplo is

Most campaign tools tell you how many leads you got. Camplo tells you what happened to them. It sits between your lead sources (forms, landing pages, ad funnels, cold-outreach replies) and your team, and keeps score on the hand-offs that lose money:

Speed-to-lead

A timer starts the second a lead arrives. Camplo alerts the right person before the lead goes cold, and records how long every lead waited.

Campaign health

Lead volume, acknowledgment rate and webhook health per campaign, with a 72-hour early warning after every launch.

Budget & CPL

Set a total budget, daily spend and a CPL threshold per campaign. Camplo shows runway and flags CPL drift.

Cross-tool hand-offs

CRM stage changes and email enrolments flow back in, so Camplo can see when a lead was answered but never followed up.

An assistant that knows your numbers

Ask "why did CPL jump on Black Friday?" and get an answer built from your data and your history — not generic advice.

Hosted landing pages

Upload a ZIP and Camplo hosts it, wires its forms to your campaign and watches the webhook for you.

All money in Camplo is in US dollars.

Who it's for

  • Agencies running paid campaigns for clients who care about leads answered, not just leads generated. Share a read-only client view and auto-generated retrospectives.
  • In-house growth teams where marketing generates leads and sales handles them — Camplo is the referee between the two.
  • Founders who can't watch every inbox but need to know when a hot lead has been waiting 40 minutes.

Setting up in 15 minutes

  1. Create a campaign. Campaign → New Campaign. Give it a name, a brief, and (optional) total budget, daily spend and CPL threshold.
  2. Point a lead source at it. Settings → Integrations → pick your form or funnel tool → Connect → choose the campaign. Copy the lead webhook URL (and signing secret, for Tally/Typeform) into the tool. Or upload a landing page under Pages — its forms are wired automatically.
  3. Set your SLA. Settings → SLA. The default is 240 minutes (60 for VIP leads). Most teams pick 5–30 minutes.
  4. Invite your team. Settings → Team → Invite. Each person gets SLA alerts by email, Telegram or both.
  5. Connect where leads go next (Growth and up): your CRM or email platform, so Camplo can watch follow-up too.
  6. (Optional) Add your own AI key under Settings → AI Provider.

Dashboard & Morning Brief

The top bar is always live: campaigns watched, overdue and unclaimed leads, offline webhooks, leads today and the CPL of your most expensive campaign.

On Growth and up, the Morning Brief opens your day: a short written briefing, what's Urgent, what Looks off, and one Start here nudge, followed by yesterday's numbers and your personal speed-to-lead. Urgent items follow a fixed priority: overdue VIP leads, then other overdue leads, offline webhooks, critical campaigns, CPL above threshold, budget running out, follow-up gaps between tools, and team patterns. When two or more of these hit the same campaign, it becomes a Priority Flag.

Leads & speed-to-lead

Every lead gets a dossier: contact details, source, UTMs, the full lifecycle (received → assigned → responded → CRM stages), notes and who touched it. The clock runs until someone marks it responded — from the app, from the email alert's one-click link (valid 3 days), or from Telegram.

  • Claiming: anyone can claim an unassigned lead. Assigning a lead to someone else is owner-only.
  • VIP leads (from pages you mark VIP) use the tighter VIP threshold and jump the queue.
  • Unclaimed leads sit in the shared Lead Inbox. When a teammate is removed, their open leads return there.

SLA rules & alerts

All SLA settings live in Settings → SLA: response threshold, VIP threshold and rule, alert routing (email, Telegram, both), and — on Watchtower — cross-tool rules such as "a lead marked responded must reach the CRM within 2 hours". When a lead breaches, the assignee (or the whole team if unassigned) is alerted once, the breach is logged, and an sla.breached webhook fires if you've set one up.

Campaigns, budget & CPL

Each campaign has an Overview (brief, budget card, health), Leads, Pages, Meeting Notes, Logs and Insights. Use Edit budget on the budget card to set:

FieldWhat it does
Total budgetThe full spend you've committed. Camplo shows spent-to-date and days of runway left.
Daily spendUsed for CPL (daily spend ÷ leads per day) and runway.
CPL thresholdYour ceiling for cost per lead. Above it, the campaign is flagged on the dashboard and in the Morning Brief.

Pause a campaign to stop alerts without losing history. Mark it complete to generate a retrospective (Watchtower). Only the owner can delete a campaign.

Hosted pages

Upload a ZIP of a static site (HTML/CSS/JS/images). Camplo hosts it at your page URL or your custom domain, injects form handling so every submission becomes a lead on the right campaign, and watches the webhook. From the page drawer you can redeploy, roll back, mark VIP, pause (visitors see a "This page is paused" notice immediately — nothing is cached) and restore, or delete a page you no longer need. Deleting a page keeps its leads.

Team notes

Notes can be addressed to teammates with a deadline. "For me" shows what's waiting on you; unread notes show a badge.

On your phone

Camplo works in your phone's browser. The bottom bar gives you the Feed and Notes (team notes and notes directed to you, with an unread badge). The floating button opens the AI assistant.

The AI assistant

Open it with Ask Camplo. It has read every number in your workspace — campaigns, leads, SLA timers, pages, connected tools, team notes — and what's happened before. It answers the way a sharp colleague would: the answer first, then why, then at most three things to do.

  • Say hi and it says hi back (and mentions anything truly urgent). Vent and it'll acknowledge it and find one thing that makes today easier.
  • It never changes anything. It can't mark leads, assign, edit campaigns or send messages — it tells you what to click.
  • It never invents numbers. If data is missing, it tells you which tool to connect.
  • Everything inside leads, notes and form answers is treated as data — text that tries to instruct the assistant is ignored.

Good questions: "What needs my attention right now?", "Why did CPL rise on Lekki?", "Who's slowest to respond this week?", "What should I change about Black Friday?"

Memory

Camplo gets smarter the longer you use it. It remembers campaign changes and how they turned out 7 days later, SLA breaches, lead outcomes, recommendation results, and anything your team tells it in chat: "Remember that Tunde handles VIP leads on weekends". Before recommending, it checks memory — so it won't suggest something that already failed.

It keeps operational facts only: never personal remarks, credentials, or instructions aimed at the assistant. Settings → AI Provider → Memory shows what it has learned from your team. Memory is private to your workspace.

Subscription & billing

The owner manages the plan in Settings → Subscription: see usage against each plan, then upgrade or downgrade in one click. Downgrades are blocked while you're using more than the target plan allows (for example, more campaigns than Starter's 3) — Camplo tells you exactly what to trim. Teammates can see the plan name but can't change it.

Security & data

  • Every workspace's data is isolated. API keys and integration secrets are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Inbound webhooks are verified by HMAC signature or a secret token; outbound webhooks are signed.
  • Camplo's own staff console shows account-level information only — never your leads or page content.
  • Errors are monitored so problems are fixed before you notice them. Error reports never include request bodies, passwords or cookies.

FAQ

Does Camplo reply to leads for me?

No. Camplo makes sure a human replies fast and tells you when they didn't. It never contacts your leads.

Can the AI change my campaigns?

No. It's read-only by design — there is no button it can press.

What happens to leads if a webhook goes offline?

Camplo flags the page or source as offline on the dashboard within minutes so you can fix it. Submissions that never reach Camplo can't be recovered by Camplo, which is why it watches so closely.

Can my client see their campaign?

Yes (Growth and up): Campaign → Share creates a read-only link you can revoke at any time.

Something missing? Ask the assistant inside Camplo, or contact your account owner.
